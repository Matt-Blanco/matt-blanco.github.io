A Conversation Between Computation

Project Type: Installation

A Computational Conversation bridges two computers released decades apart from one another. A 1987 Apple IIGS and a 2024 Macbook Air.

With one computer running Eliza, a psychoanalytical chatbot algorithm from the 1960s, and Claude Haiku 5.5 a contemporary AI model. The two computers converse with one another with the Apple IIGS initiating the conversation with a message: "How do you do. Please tell me your problems.". The two devices proceed to converse with one another.
